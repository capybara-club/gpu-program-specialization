# Concurrent Compile and GPU Execution

## Configuration

This benchmark measures both static-column materialize and SSE kernels on an
NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 with an AMD Ryzen 9 9900X:

- 24 OpenMP compiler workers
- 24 modules
- 8 kernels/module
- 128 ASTs/kernel
- 1,024 ASTs/module and 24,576 ASTs/campaign
- 8 pre-created CUDA streams
- 8,192 rows/CTA and 128 threads/CTA
- 1,024 through 262,144 rows/AST

Completed modules enter a ready queue. The GPU consumer waits on the previous
module's all-stream stop event, measures it, unloads it, loads the next
completed module, and launches its kernels. Inputs and targets remain resident
on the GPU. AST construction, cold template creation, inspection, template
copying, allocation, transfers, and correctness checks are outside the
pipeline timer.

The measured data and graph are
[`pipeline_rtx5090_2026-07-26.csv`](pipeline_rtx5090_2026-07-26.csv) and
[`pipeline_rtx5090_2026-07-26.svg`](pipeline_rtx5090_2026-07-26.svg).

## Compile Throughput

Values are median AST/s across the six row counts. Row count does not
participate in compilation; its repetitions provide independent samples.

| Backend | Materialize ALU | Materialize MUFU | SSE ALU | SSE MUFU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CUDA O0 | 12.45K | 4.40K | 2.26K | 1.62K |
| CUDA O1 | 11.74K | 4.21K | 2.42K | 1.68K |
| PTX O0 | 86.91K | 39.93K | 5.12K | 4.29K |
| PTX O1 | 62.69K | 26.13K | 8.27K | 6.40K |
| CUBIN | **30.65M** | **16.79M** | **20.53M** | **10.88M** |

These are measured 24-worker wall rates. `compile_work_seconds` in the CSV is
the sum of the per-module CPU compile durations.

transitions. Pipeline throughput includes concurrent compilation, module
transitions, and runtime.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CUDA O0 | 3.360e11 | 3.264e9 | 3.243e11 | 1.148e9 |
| CUDA O1 | 3.222e11 | 3.054e9 | 3.282e11 | 1.112e9 |
| PTX O0 | 3.292e11 | 2.177e10 | **3.323e11** | 1.012e10 |
| PTX O1 | 3.220e11 | 1.592e10 | 3.295e11 | 6.752e9 |
| CUBIN | **3.460e11** | **1.628e11** | 3.144e11 | **1.478e11** |

## SSE Endpoint

| Backend | ALU runtime | ALU pipeline | MUFU runtime | MUFU pipeline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CUDA O0 | 4.628e11 | 5.986e8 | 1.947e11 | 4.271e8 |
| CUDA O1 | 2.400e12 | 6.249e8 | 9.014e11 | 4.325e8 |
| PTX O0 | 2.370e11 | 1.333e9 | 1.314e11 | 1.114e9 |
| PTX O1 | **2.503e12** | 2.151e9 | **9.245e11** | 1.673e9 |
| CUBIN | 1.366e12 | **2.912e11** | 3.579e11 | **1.835e11** |

Rates are row evaluations per second. Targets do not multiply the SSE count.
O1 native compilation produces the fastest SSE schedules. Direct CUBIN
specialization remains on the compile/run frontier because it produces AST
modules millions of times per second while retaining competitive kernel
throughput.

ALU verification uses tight absolute and relative tolerances. MUFU verification
uses wider tolerances for native approximate reciprocal, square-root, sine, and
cosine operations. A previously failing MUFU seed produced exactly the same GPU
result through CUDA, PTX, and direct CUBIN, confirming that the discrepancy was
GPU approximation relative to the CPU `libm` oracle rather than divergent
lowering.
