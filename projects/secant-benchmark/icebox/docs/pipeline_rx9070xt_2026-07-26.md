# Concurrent Compile and GPU Execution

## Configuration

This benchmark measures static-column materialize and SSE kernels on a Radeon
RX 9070 XT (`gfx1201`, ROCm 7.1) with an AMD Ryzen 9 7900X:

- 24 OpenMP compiler workers
- 24 modules
- 8 kernels/module
- 128 ASTs/kernel
- 1,024 ASTs/module and 24,576 ASTs/campaign
- 8 pre-created HIP streams
- 8,192 rows/CTA and 128 threads/CTA
- 1,024 through 262,144 rows/AST

Completed modules enter a ready queue. The GPU consumer waits on the previous
module's all-stream stop event, measures it, unloads it, loads the next
completed module, and launches its kernels. Inputs and targets remain resident
on the GPU. AST construction, cold template creation, inspection, template
copying, allocation, transfers, and correctness checks are outside the
pipeline timer.

The measured data and graph are
[`pipeline_rx9070xt_2026-07-26.csv`](pipeline_rx9070xt_2026-07-26.csv) and
[`pipeline_rx9070xt_2026-07-26.svg`](pipeline_rx9070xt_2026-07-26.svg).

## Compile Throughput

Values are median AST/s across the six row counts. Row count does not
participate in compilation; its repetitions provide independent samples.

| Backend | Materialize ALU | Materialize MUFU | SSE ALU | SSE MUFU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HIP O1 | 1.61K | 702 | 218 | 201 |
| HSACO | **15.77M** | **7.48M** | **14.30M** | **7.70M** |

These are measured 24-worker wall rates. `compile_work_seconds` in the CSV is
the sum of the per-module CPU compile durations.

## 262,144-Row Endpoint

Rates are row evaluations per second. Runtime excludes compilation and module
transitions. Pipeline throughput includes concurrent compilation, module
transitions, and runtime.

| Shape | AST mode | Backend | Runtime | Pipeline |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Materialize | ALU | HIP O1 | 5.055e10 | 4.207e8 |
| Materialize | ALU | HSACO | **6.590e10** | **4.916e10** |
| Materialize | MUFU | HIP O1 | 5.161e10 | 1.837e8 |
| Materialize | MUFU | HSACO | **5.873e10** | **4.584e10** |
| SSE | ALU | HIP O1 | **1.174e11** | 5.720e7 |
| SSE | ALU | HSACO | 4.871e10 | **3.579e10** |
| SSE | MUFU | HIP O1 | **9.234e10** | 5.248e7 |
| SSE | MUFU | HSACO | 4.114e10 | **3.206e10** |

All 48 measured cells ran on the GPU and passed comparison with the CPU
interpreter. HIPRTC created the requested OpenMP worker threads but sustained
about one CPU core during the native HIP compile phases. Its compile calls are
therefore effectively serialized in this in-process configuration. Direct
HSACO specialization does not invoke HIPRTC in the hot path.

## HIP O0 Boundary

HIP O0 is not plotted because its materialize MUFU form did not produce a
runnable equivalent shape at this packing. It generated about 35 KiB of
private storage per thread and failed with
`HSA_STATUS_ERROR_OUT_OF_RESOURCES`. The sweep does not silently reduce
packing, row count, or stream count to make that configuration pass.
